# Clarify the WEPA.care Splash Story

## Goal
Make the opening understandable to a technical judge with no travel-nursing background: Puerto Rico hospitals need nurses, qualified U.S. nurses take temporary assignments away from home, and tourism benefits help make local-pay opportunities attractive.

## Changes
- Replace the current opening headline with a direct problem statement led by **“Puerto Rico needs nurses.”**
- Add one short plain-English explanation of a travel nurse and why Puerto Rico faces a harder recruiting challenge.
- Present WEPA.care as the solution connecting hospitals, U.S. nurses, and tourism partners so assignments combine meaningful work with lodging, travel, and local experiences.
- Keep **“Come for the Island. Care for its People.”** as the emotional brand payoff rather than asking it to explain the product.
- Preserve the existing three audience cards, role icons, jingle transition, visual system, and mobile layout.
- Add an equivalent Spanish translation with the same direct, civilian-friendly meaning.

## Message hierarchy
```text
Puerto Rico needs nurses.
Plain-English explanation of the travel-nurse challenge.
How WEPA connects all three participants to solve it.
Come for the Island. Care for its People.
Choose the relevant journey.
```

## Validation
- Confirm the full opening message fits without crowding or overlap on phone and desktop.
- Confirm English and Spanish communicate the same problem and solution.
- Confirm all three role cards and the jingle entry continue to work.
