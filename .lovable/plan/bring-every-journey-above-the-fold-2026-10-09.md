# Bring every journey above the fold

## Goal
Keep the splash content and visual impact intact while making all three audience choices immediately visible on a typical laptop and much earlier on a phone.

## Desktop and tablet

- Replace the single tall vertical stack with a two-column composition beneath the logo:
  - **Left:** headline, explainer, tagline, four stats, result line, and travel-nurse solution punch.
  - **Right:** “Find your place” plus three compact journey doors stacked vertically.
- Keep the west-coast image full-screen and preserve the current deep-ocean wash.
- Keep every word, number, supplied icon, logo, color, and interaction unchanged.
- Make the journey doors shorter horizontal entries: role icon, audience/title/copy, and arrow. They remain visually substantial without using the current tall card height.
- At the current 1024×790 judge viewport, all three doors should be fully visible without scrolling.

## Phone

- Keep the story in one column, but place the journey choices immediately after the bold travel-nurse solution.
- Compress spacing and use the existing horizontal door format so the first journey choice appears in the initial screen and the remaining choices require only a short, obvious scroll.
- Preserve the 2×2 stat grid, readable labels, and full copy rather than shrinking text to an uncomfortable size.

## Validation

- Check English and Spanish at 1024×790, wide desktop, and phone widths.
- Confirm all three desktop doors are above the fold, phone content remains readable, nothing overlaps, and the jingle still starts from every door.
- Run the focused tests and confirm the preview build is clean.
